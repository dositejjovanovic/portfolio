## Introduction

As Board Member & International Officer — Union of High School Students of Serbia, I see every activity as part of a larger responsibility: helping ensure that students can participate meaningfully in the issues that shape their lives.

## Context

UNSS brings together high school students around advocacy, participation and the improvement of student life. Its work depends on young people who are willing to organise, listen and act collectively.

## My role

My role on the board involves contributing to the organisation’s direction while staying connected to the experiences and needs of students. It combines practical responsibility with the privilege of representing a wider community.

## Experience

Board work has taught me the importance of consistency: preparing carefully, communicating clearly and supporting a team through both visible activities and the planning that happens behind them.

## Impact

Strong student organisations make participation more accessible and advocacy more sustained. They give students a platform to move from individual concerns to shared priorities and action.

## Conclusion

I am committed to continuing this work with UNSS and to helping build a student movement that is informed, inclusive and ready to contribute nationally and internationally.
