I took part in a consultation process with civil society organizations on the drafting of the Program for the Protection of Children from Violence.

Representatives of civil society, including the Union of Secondary School Students of Serbia, were invited to contribute by identifying key challenges, assessing existing mechanisms, and outlining expectations for the new strategic framework.

The discussion brought together a wide range of stakeholders, including SOS Children’s Villages Serbia, the Association of Social Workers of Serbia, the Parents Association of Serbia, UNICEF, the Center for Missing and Exploited Children, and the Network of Organizations for Children of Serbia, among others. Through both plenary dialogue and thematic working groups, we addressed specific issues and challenges faced by the sector.

The consultation provided a valuable platform for constructive and evidence-based input. I am confident that the recommendations put forward will be duly considered and reflected in the final program, contributing to a more coherent and effective system of child protection.
