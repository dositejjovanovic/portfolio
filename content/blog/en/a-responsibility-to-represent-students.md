## Introduction

I was deeply honoured by an opportunity that reaffirmed why I care about student representation: young people deserve to be present in the conversations that define their education and future.

## Context

Representation is not a title alone. It is a commitment to listen, report back, build trust and make sure that a wide range of student experiences is reflected in the work being done.

## My role

I took part with a strong sense of responsibility to my peers and to the organisations that make student participation possible. My aim was to contribute thoughtfully and learn from the experience.

## Experience

The opportunity showed me how much preparation and humility matter. Representing others requires both the confidence to speak and the discipline to remain open to what others need and know.

## Impact

Every serious representation opportunity builds capacity. It gives young people experience with dialogue, advocacy and accountability—skills that strengthen both schools and democratic culture.

## Conclusion

I am motivated to carry this responsibility forward by keeping student voice at the centre of my leadership and project work.
