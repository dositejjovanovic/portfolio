From May 8th to 10th, I had the incredible opportunity to participate in the 67th Regional Session of EYP Serbia held in Belgrade. 

During the session, I was a delegate in the European Youth Parliament Serbia AFET Committee, where we worked on the topic “Dragon at the Gates” addressing the growing influence of Chinese investments in Serbia and the Western Balkans, as well as the challenges they pose for transparency, economic independence, environmental standards, and Serbia’s long-term European integration prospects. Together, we developed a comprehensive resolution focused on balancing economic development with the protection of democratic values, fair competition, labour rights, and alignment with EU standards.

The session itself was an unforgettable experience. On the first day, we participated in teambuilding activities that helped us connect, communicate, and create an amazing atmosphere within the committee. The second day was dedicated to committee work, where we spent hours debating, negotiating, and shaping our ideas into a final resolution. Finally, on the last day, we took part in the General Assembly, where our committee defended the resolution in front of all delegates. I am proud to say that our resolution was successfully passed.

What made this experience truly special were the people. The delegates in my committee were incredibly supportive, and inspiring, while our chairpersons created an environment where everyone felt heard and motivated to contribute. Beyond the debates and formal procedures, the session was filled with meaningful conversations, new friendships, unforgettable moments, and a shared passion for creating positive change.

Participating in this session strengthened my public speaking, critical thinking, teamwork, and diplomacy skills, but most importantly, it reminded me how powerful young people can be when they work together toward a common goal.
