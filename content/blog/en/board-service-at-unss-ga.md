As a Board Member of Unija srednjoškolaca Srbije, I had the opportunity to participate in the Extraordinary General Assembly, where we discussed numerous amendments to internal acts and important positions of the entire network.

During the session, we also presented the Board’s report covering the previous six months, reflecting on the work, initiatives, and progress achieved in that period.

Grateful to be part of a team dedicated to strengthening student representation, youth participation, and cooperation among high school students across Serbia.
